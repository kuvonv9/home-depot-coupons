## Can you stack a Home Depot coupon with cashback?

Sometimes, but do not treat stacking as unconditional.

The normal structure is:

- A Home Depot coupon or sale lowers the checkout price.
- Cashback is calculated on the eligible net purchase amount.
- The retailer later confirms or rejects the transaction.
- The cashback platform credits the amount after the confirmation period.

ShopBack’s Home Depot guide says cashback may be layered on top of a Home Depot coupon, Special Buy price, or Pro Xtra discount. It also warns that category exclusions and promotion-specific terms apply.

The safest combinations are usually:

- Home Depot sale price plus tracked cashback
- Home Depot email or app offer plus tracked cashback
- Special Buy price plus tracked cashback

The riskier combinations are:

- Unlisted coupon codes from random coupon websites
- Gift card purchases
- Tool or truck rentals
- Installation services
- Orders containing excluded brands or categories
- Purchases made after clicking another cashback extension
- Orders completed in a different browser session

If the cashback matters, do not open several shopping extensions at once. Multiple referral systems competing for the same transaction can make tracking less reliable.

## Common Home Depot coupon mistakes

### Using an expired code

Many coupon pages keep old codes visible after the original promotion has ended. If Home Depot does not accept the code in the cart, do not assume there is a hidden formatting issue. The code may simply be expired or restricted.

### Ignoring the minimum purchase

A $5 discount on a $50 purchase is effectively a 10% saving only when the full purchase qualifies. If the offer applies to a $50 threshold and you spend $150, the percentage benefit is smaller.

### Buying an excluded brand

Major appliances, premium tools, gift cards, services, and selected national brands are often treated differently from ordinary merchandise. Read the exclusions before building your cart around a coupon.

### Forgetting delivery and service fees

A product discount may not reduce delivery, installation, haul-away, or other service charges. Compare the final order total, not only the product subtotal.

### Opening the Home Depot app after activating cashback

If the cashback route is activated in a browser and the order is completed in another app, tracking may fail. Start over through the same route if the purchase flow changes.

### Returning part of the order

A partial return can reduce the confirmed cashback. Keep the order email and return documentation until both the refund and cashback status are settled.
